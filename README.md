# ITAI1378-Final-Project

🚀 Final Project: The Build (Proof of Concept)
ITAI 1378 Computer Vision and AI 💯 Worth: 35% of your final grade 📅 Due: See the Canvas due date for this assignment

🎬 What This Is
At the Midterm you delivered the Blueprint. Now you deliver the Build: a working proof of concept of your computer vision application, demonstrated live or on video.

A proof of concept does not need to be perfect or production ready. It needs to work: real input goes in, your system processes it, real output comes out, and you can show it happening.

Your Blueprint was a plan, and plans change. Changing models, shrinking scope, or switching datasets is normal engineering. What matters is that you explain what changed and why.

📦 What to Submit (3 things)
1. Final Presentation Slides (PDF or PPTX)
10 to 12 slides (structure below)
File naming, match it exactly:
Individual: pdf (example: FP_Smith_John_ITAI1378.pdf)
Team: pdf (example: FP_VisionSquad_Smith_ITAI1378.pdf)
Teams submit once: one member uploads for the whole team, with their name as the submitter in the file name
On campus sections: 10 minute live presentation with a live demo. Have your backup video ready in case of technical problems.
Online asynchronous sections: 10 minute recorded presentation covering your slides and demo. Post the link in the Canvas comments.
2. Demo Video Link (Uploaded to GitHub or added in the slides )
3 to 5 minutes showing your system actually working
YouTube (Unlisted) or Google Drive (Anyone with link can view)
Everyone submits a demo video It is your backup and your proof.
A good demo video: - Shows real input going in and real output coming out - Includes your narration explaining what is happening - Shows at least one case where the system struggles or fails, and you explain why. Honest engineers show failure cases. It earns credit, it does not lose credit.

3. GitHub Repository Link (Paste in the slides)
Make the repo public, or add me as a collaborator
